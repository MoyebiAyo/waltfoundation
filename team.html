Princess Bridget Tola Ogundipe

Leads the Foundation's vision to restore dignity, safety and opportunity to women, children and widows across rural Nigeria.

President

Architect Adewale Sunday Ogundipe

Engineer Ogundipe Tunbi Ajanlekoko

Leads the Foundation's IT and programme management and heads its administration — keeping records, systems and outreaches organised, documented and running.

Chief Adeleye Bankole Samuel

Ayodele Oluwasegun Moyebi

Royal patronage

A patron of national standing.

Grand Patron · Ooni of Ife

His Imperial Majesty King Babatunde Adeyeye Enitan Oluwatunmise Ogunwusi

The Ooni of Ife — paramount traditional ruler of Ile-Ife and spiritual leader of the Yoruba people worldwide — lends the Foundation a cultural legitimacy and community standing that opens doors across Nigeria, strengthening its work in reaching underserved populations.
