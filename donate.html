What your support funds

Four programmes of impact.

  • Education & Girl-Child Support — schooling and protection pathways for girls and under-resourced mothers.
  • Child Welfare & Protection — healthcare, shelter, education and safe recovery for vulnerable children.
  • Feeding & Community Relief — food packs and relief for households in acute need.
  • Women & Widow Empowerment — helping widows stabilize households and rebuild livelihoods.
