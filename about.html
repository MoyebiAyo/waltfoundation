About the Foundation

Grassroots trust, institutional legitimacy.

Incorporated June 7, 2023. Operating across rural Nigeria, with state-level certification in Osun State.

Executive summary

Restoring dignity to the most vulnerable.

Walts Charity and Empowerment Foundation is a duly registered Nigerian non-profit organization committed to restoring dignity, safety, and opportunity to the most vulnerable members of society. Operating primarily across rural communities, the Foundation serves under-age girls, widows, orphans, and survivors of abuse — groups too often overlooked by mainstream development efforts.

Anchored in full legal, financial, and state-level regulatory compliance, Walts Charity combines grassroots proximity with institutional credibility, positioning it as a trusted vehicle for donors, development partners, and community stakeholders seeking measurable, values-driven impact.

Our Vision

A future where no woman, child, or widow in rural Nigeria is left without protection, education, or the means to rebuild her life.

Our Mission

To uplift disadvantaged individuals — especially women, children, and orphans — through care, education, and empowerment programs that break generational cycles of poverty, abuse, and neglect.

Strategic focus areas

Four pillars of impact.

Each programme combines immediate protection with long-term, sustainable pathways.

01

Girl-Child & Maternal Support

Direct intervention for under-age girls, survivors of sexual violence and forced marriage, and under-resourced mothers in rural settings — combining protection with pathways to education and self-sufficiency.

02

Child Welfare & Development

Access to healthcare, shelter, and education for sick, impoverished, abandoned, and orphaned children, addressing both immediate needs and long-term development.

03

Child Protection

Removing children from abusive environments and providing continued access to schooling and emotional/psychosocial support during recovery and reintegration.

04

Widow Empowerment

Financial and emotional support for young widows facing sudden loss, helping them stabilize households and rebuild sustainable livelihoods.

Governance & Compliance

Trust, verified.

This layered compliance — federal incorporation, anti-money-laundering registration, and state-level operating certification — directly addresses the due-diligence checklists most institutional donors and government partners require before releasing funds. Strategic positioning

Where grassroots trust meets institutional legitimacy.

Walts Charity and Empowerment Foundation stands at the intersection of grassroots trust and institutional legitimacy. Its combination of legal standing, regulatory compliance, and deep community access makes it well-positioned to scale impact through strategic partnerships — offering funders and collaborators a credible, on-the-ground channel for humanitarian investment in some of Nigeria's most underserved communities.
