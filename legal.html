Governance

The Foundation is governed by an appointed Board of Trustees responsible for strategic direction and fiduciary accountability. Current trusteeship includes the Founder, Princess Bridget Tola Ogundipe, and the President, Architect Adewale Sunday Ogundipe. King Babatunde Adeyeye Enitan Oluwatunmise Ogunwusi serves as the Foundation's Patron. Ogundipe Tunbi Ajanlekoko serves as Secretary, IT & Programme Manager.
